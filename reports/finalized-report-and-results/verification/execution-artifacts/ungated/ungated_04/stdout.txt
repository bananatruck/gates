EXPERIMENT EXPLANATION:
This controlled data-efficiency experiment trains multinomial logistic
regression with full-batch gradient descent (lr=0.1, zero initialization,
1500 steps) on 25% of the 400-sample training pool (100 samples) versus the
full pool (400 samples). The fixed 200-sample held-out test set is identical
for both arms, so any difference in test accuracy is caused purely by the
amount of labelled training data. The headline metric
eff.efficiency_ratio = eff.acc_at_25 / eff.acc_at_100 states what fraction
of the full-data test accuracy is already reached with one quarter of the
training data.
